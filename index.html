Our Story

Why Dough began as a simple passion project in a home kitchen, inspired by the joy of baking for family and friends and fueled by late-night cookie cravings. We believed that everyone deserves a truly exceptional cookie experience.

What started as sharing cookies with loved ones quickly grew into something more. The name “Why Dough?” reflects our philosophy: why settle for ordinary when you can have extraordinary? Why not indulge in treats made with care, creativity, and premium ingredients?

Today, we continue to dough it better—one perfectly baked, soft, chewy cookie at a time. Each batch is still crafted with the same love and attention that started it all.
