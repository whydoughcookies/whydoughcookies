Our Story

Why Dough began as a simple passion project in a home kitchen, fueled by late-night cookie cravings and the belief that everyone deserves a truly exceptional cookie experience.

What started as sharing cookies with friends and family quickly grew into something more. The name "Why Dough?" reflects our philosophy: why settle for ordinary when you can have extraordinary? Why not indulge in something made with care, creativity, and the finest ingredients?

Today, we continue to dough it better—one perfectly baked, soft, chewy cookie at a time. Each batch is still made with the same love and attention that started it all.
